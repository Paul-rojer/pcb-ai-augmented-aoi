AOI PRO

"Inspection you can trust — auto-clear the good boards, escalate only what truly needs a human."

AOI PRO builds decision-support AI that sits on top of existing AOI machines. Instead of replacing the line, our system acts as a second opinion: it confirms confident passes, flags genuine anomalies, and routes only the uncertain cases to operators — cutting manual review while never letting a defect through unchecked.

The model evolved from an EfficientNet classifier — which over-fit to lighting and conveyor rails and could not confidently pass good boards — to a robust golden-template registration engine with per-board references, ECC sub-pixel alignment, and AOI/AI consensus. The current release auto-detects board type, localizes defects with a per-pixel anomaly heatmap, and is engineered so a good board is never auto-rejected.
